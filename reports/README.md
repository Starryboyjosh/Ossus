# Reports

Committed architecture, security, and WAVE closure reports may be stored here once they exist.

Each WAVE also has a technical and practical reader summary under
`docs/implementation/08-operations/wave-summaries/`; the final summary links
the reviewed evidence and Closure Agent decision required by
[`docs/AGENT_AUTHORITY.md`](../docs/AGENT_AUTHORITY.md).

Local generated output belongs under `reports/local/`, which is ignored.
